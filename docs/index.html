A smaller, safer production loop

The handoff is part of the product.

A starter should make the next decision easier. That means describing what works, what was checked, and what still needs production access or client approval.

  1. 01

    Scope one outcome

    Agree the exact page, edit, or workflow before work begins.

  2. 02

    Build the smallest real version

    Use a dependable stack and avoid decorative dependencies.

  3. 03

    Check the relevant layer

    Test behavior, source, and delivery claims in proportion to risk.

  4. 04

    Name the production boundary

    Credentials, legal copy, paid media, and live integrations stay approval-gated.
